About the series

About BayesCompJp

BayesCompJp promotes collaboration among researchers in Bayesian statistics, statistical computing, and related fields, primarily in Japan. Seminars and workshops are held periodically in hybrid form. Talks may be in English or Japanese; all times are shown in JST.

BayesCompJpは、日本を主な対象地域として、ベイズ統計学・統計計算・関連分野の研究者の協力関係を促進するためのセミナーとワークショップを開催しています。講演は英語または日本語で行われ、時刻は日本標準時で表示します。

Organised by Kengo Kamatani (Institute of Statistical Mathematics) with colleagues. The series has received support from ISM cooperative programme 2023-ISMCRP-1012, JSPS KAKENHI 21K18589 and 20H04149, and the JST CREST project on statistics for stochastic systems.
